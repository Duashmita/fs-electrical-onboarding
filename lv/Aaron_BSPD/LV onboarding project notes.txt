   * Stages of the BSPD
      * BUFFER
         * Make a copy of the sensor voltage 
            * Makes it so it doesn't overload the sensor
      * Opens
         * Compares the buffered voltage to the threshold it is allowed
            * Will signal to open circuit if not within threshold
      * Hard breaking and high current
         * Determines if the car is “hard” braking
         * Determines if motor is still pulling high current
         * Doesn't put them together
      * Shorts
         * Compares sensor outputs and short circuit threshold
      * Amplifiers
         * Make the sensors outputs more resistant to noise
