# Speakers

Reqs:
- Must capture clear human voices, not necessarily good bass
- Can be heard over noise without unsafe listening levels.
- Small physical size, or if larger, whats the advantage?
- Low power consumption - easy to drive using a small amplifier(?)
- Survives vibration and repeated use

A larger speaker can move more air, easier to reproduce low frequencies and maybe create greater acoustic output, but for a helmet communication system we care more abt the frequencies that make speech understandable, so example provided wont work
