### Key terms
1. voltage (V, volts (V)): electrical pressure inside of conductor (think volume).^1f22f6
	- also called electrical potential difference
		- this is because voltage is always a difference between two points
2. current (I, amperes (A)): rate of charge flowing past a point ^936185
3. resistance (R, ohms (Omega/horseshoe)): opposition to current flow ^be79bd
4. [[Circuits 101#^9c02c6|Ohm's Law]]: Rule that links all of the above together ^08b8c1
5. [[Circuits 101|Circuits]]: closed loop that lets electricity run through
	1. analog: smooth signals (think of sin/cos wave functions)
		- very susceptible to noise & signal degradation when through amplifier
	2. digital: high/low voltage, binary signals (on and off)
		- immune to small noise fluctuations

### Important components
1. resistor: restricts current to protect parts
2. capacitor: stores & releases electrical energy temporarily (mini battery)
3. diodes: one way gate for current ^21303d
4. transistors: amplifies or is logic gate ^ef2f7d
	- digital transistors: acts like logic gate (receives some voltage and opens)
	- analog transistors: uses power supply to copy weaker signal
