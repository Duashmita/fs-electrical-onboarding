### General Information
#### 1. Types of solder
1. leaded solder (lower fixed melting point, but is toxic)
2. lead free solder (has melting range, not point)
#### 2. Flux
1. removes oxidation (stops oxygen from touching the metal)
2. ensures strong bond
3. most solder wires already have this inside
#### 3. Solder wick braid
1. braided copper wire
2. used to absorb unwanted solder
	solder travels up the braid when heated (like water)\
	acts very similar to a sponge, except you cant just dump away the solder
	you have to cut off the used part
3. usually coated in flux
#### 4. Soldering splint / helping hands
1. just to hold things steady
#### 5. Safety Guide
1. do not touch soldering iron (up to 400c/800f)
	don't let anything touch it
	however, the globules that fall down cool down immediately mid air (mostly)
2. fume extractors are necessary
	you can make this DIY

### Soldering Steps
#### 1. Use helping hands to hold the PCB
	can also use the solder splints
#### 2. One hand holds the iron, the other holds the solder
#### 3. Put some flux on the contacts (place you want to solder)
	solder always wants to bond with copper
	only stops because of oxidized copper layer (thats why use flux)
#### 4. Turn on soldering iron and fume extractor
	you can test if iron is hot by using solder on tip
	coat the iron tip with solder or tip cleaner to protect it
	this will stop it from corrosion or oxidation
#### 5. Apply iron to contact and component pin for a few seconds
	heat it up evenly to accept solder
	if too cold, solder will ball up
	if hot enough, it will look like hersheys (tapered)
#### 6. Clean the area with isopropyl alcohol
	flux is corrosive, even if they claim that they are "no clean"
	alc first, then cotton swab to wipe gently

### Desoldering Guide
#### 1. Evenly heat up the solder & copper
	just like soldering but inverted
#### 2. Put some flux on the braid
	this is to remove the oxidized barrier
	solder is attracted to copper
#### 3. Place braid on solder and put iron on braid & solder
	drag the braid along the solder to absorb it like a sponge
