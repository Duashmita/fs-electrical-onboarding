Link to microphone: https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/same-sky-formerly-cui-devices/CMB-6544PF/1869982

CMB-6544PF -> A microphone

I would place the microphone and speaker inside the helmet.

I would also need a connector for the microphone and speaker. The specific connector I would use is CONN JACK STEREO 3.5MM TH R/A. 

DigiKey Link to connector: https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/kycon-inc/STX-3000/9975995

I think the main challenge a microphone in this situation would face is that the signals might not be clear because the audio might not send enough frequency. Air pressure might also prevent clear signals. A small driver should be fine.
