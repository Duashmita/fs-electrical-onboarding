## The suspension chord:

R = pL/A -> (Resistivity*Length)/(Area) = Resistance
- Area = length * width


- Information found in the JLC Website.
p = resistivity of the copper: 1.724 \times 10^{-8}
R = 350 ohms


Width = 3 mil = 0.0762 mm
- I used an online conversion calculator to do this.

l = 12 *10^-6
Length = (R*A)/p

Length = 18.56 meters

I attempted to get it to 18560 mm, but I was not able to because it would be difficult to fit all of that into the board.
